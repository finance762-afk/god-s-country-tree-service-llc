## 5. Blog topics for P1 BLOG BOT (checked against all 14 posts and every service page)
**1. "Laurel Oak or Live Oak? How to Tell, and Why It Changes What You Do With the Tree"**
- Target query: *laurel oak vs live oak* (secondary: *laurel oak lifespan florida*)
- Angle: telling the two apart in a DeLand yard (leaves, bark, form, which stays evergreen), and why laurel oaks get assessed for decline sooner. Species ID for homeowners. It's not a general hazard checklist (that's the signs-of-a-dangerous-tree post) or a planting guide.
- Internal links: /services/dead-hazardous-tree-removal/ (primary), /services/certified-arborist-services/, and the signs-of-a-dangerous-tree post.
- Original proof: side-by-side leaf and bark photos from Caleb's jobs, a decay cross-section from a real laurel oak removal (city and month), and a quote from Caleb on what he checks. Cite UF/IFAS species fact sheets; don't invent lifespan figures.

**2. "Why Is My Pine Tree Turning Brown? A Volusia County Homeowner's Checklist"**
- Target query: *pine tree turning brown florida*
- Angle: sorting it by pattern (whole crown, top-down, scattered branches), what to look for on the trunk (pitch tubes, boring dust), why nearby pines can be at risk, and when it's time to call. Covers no cost or permit topics.
- Internal links: /services/dead-hazardous-tree-removal/ (primary), /services/tree-removal/.
- Original proof: close-up photos from real jobs (pitch tubes, bark galleries), and one dated job story with the town. Cite UF/IFAS and the Florida Forest Service on pine beetles. Use pine-removal counts only if job records exist.

**3. "Tree Roots Lifting Your Driveway or Sidewalk: Options Before Removal"**
- Target query: *tree roots lifting driveway*
- Angle: what can be done short of removal (root pruning risks, root barriers, redoing the slab), why cutting major roots can destabilize a tree, and when removal is the honest answer. The dangerous-tree post only mentions root damage briefly; this is a distinct homeowner question.
- Internal links: /services/tree-removal/ (primary), /services/certified-arborist-services/, and the tree-removal-cost post.
- Original proof: photos of real lifted slabs and exposed roots, one job where Caleb advised against removal and why, and a quote from Caleb. Ask P1 CUSTO BOT for real customer questions on this topic.

Topics I avoided: palm trimming, stump grinding and land clearing. Those should be service pages (fix #5); a post on them would compete with those pages.
